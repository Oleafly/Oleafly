# Sediment transport

Bedload grows nonlinearly with shear stress[^shear] and depends on grain shape [@meyer2019, p. 12; see also @lopez_2021].
The threshold is often written as $\tau_c = \theta_c (\rho_s - \rho) g D$ where $D$ is grain size.

Shields parameter
:   A dimensionless shear stress used to compare flows.

Critical slope
:   The bed slope at which grains begin to roll.

<details>
<summary>Derivation sketch</summary>

Balance the drag on a single grain against its submerged weight.

</details>
