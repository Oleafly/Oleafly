\section{Ethics and risk management}
\label{sec:risk}

\subsection{Ethics}

\proj{} processes identifiers from three national registries covering approximately 24 million
people, none of whom will be contacted and none of whom consented to this specific study. That is
the central ethical fact of the project and it shapes every design decision below.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.5cm} L{5.3cm} L{6.0cm}@{}}
  \toprule
  Issue & Why it arises & How \proj{} handles it \\
  \midrule
  Processing without individual consent &
  Registry data are collected under statute, not consent, and re-consenting 24 million people is
  not possible &
  Public interest basis assessed per custodian, with an independent lay panel reviewing every
  research question before extraction \\
  \addlinespace
  Re-identification of an individual &
  Any linkage raises the identifiability of the resulting record &
  No party ever holds the join. The analyst receives aggregates and a certificate, never a linked
  row. Enclave egress is reviewed by two people \\
  \addlinespace
  Harm to small subgroups &
  Linkage error concentrates in migrants and in people who change names, and so would any published
  subgroup finding &
  Subgroup results are published only with the linkage-error bound attached, which is a scientific
  requirement of \ref{obj:inference} and also an ethical one \\
  \addlinespace
  Dual use of the attack work &
  \ref{wp:encoding} improves attacks on encodings that are deployed today &
  Responsible disclosure to affected custodians 120 days before publication, coordinated through
  the governance board in \ref{wp:governance} \\
  \addlinespace
  Scope creep in the access agreement &
  A signed agreement invites questions it was not written for &
  The tripartite agreement enumerates permitted analyses. Anything outside it returns to the lay
  panel and to the custodians \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

  \ref{wp:encoding} & M & H &
  The 4 point F1 target is the ambition. At 8 points the project still succeeds, because
  \ref{obj:theory} lets the analyst price the loss instead of hiding it \\
  \addlinespace
  R3 & Bounds are not sharp enough to be useful &
  \ref{wp:theory} & L & H &
  Fall back to computable but conservative bounds plus a simulation-calibrated diagnostic. The
  deliverable becomes a tool rather than a theorem \\
  \addlinespace
  R4 & Secure comparison too slow at 8 million records &
  \ref{wp:protocol} & M & M &
  Blocking under encryption is the planned route. If it underperforms, reduce to a stratified
  sample of 2 million per custodian, which still supports MS5 \\
  \addlinespace
  R5 & An attack breaks our own construction after publication &
  \ref{wp:encoding} & L & H &
  Treated as a result, not a failure. The audit schedule in Section~\ref{sec:budget} is timed to
  find this before deployment rather than after \\
  \addlinespace
  R6 & Loss of the research software engineer &
  \ref{wp:protocol} & M & H &
  Single greatest personnel risk. Mitigated by a written protocol specification at M20, before the
  implementation, and by a six-month overlap in the recruitment plan \\
  \addlinespace
  R7 & Regulatory change during the project &
  \ref{wp:governance} & M & M &
  The architecture already exceeds current requirements by never centralising the join. Legal
  adviser retained across all five years for exactly this \\
  \addlinespace
  R8 & Reanalyses contradict published findings &
  \ref{wp:inference} & H & L &
  Expected and desirable. Original authors are contacted before submission and offered the
