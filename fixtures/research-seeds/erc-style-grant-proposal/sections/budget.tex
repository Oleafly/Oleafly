\ref{wp:protocol} is an engineering effort that a doctoral student cannot carry, which is why a

\subsection{Notes on the larger items}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Secure compute enclave, 192{,}000.} The custodians will not permit linkage on shared
    infrastructure. The year 1 figure buys the hardware; later years are operation, certification,
    and one refresh cycle.
  \item \textbf{Custodian access fees, 120{,}000.} Each of the three custodians charges a cost
    recovery fee per extraction. The profile assumes two extractions per custodian across the
    project, with the last in year 5 for the reanalyses in \ref{wp:inference}.
  \item \textbf{External security audit, 56{,}000.} Two independent audits, one after D2.1 and one
    before the three-custodian deployment. An unaudited protocol will not be approved by the
    custodians, so this is a delivery requirement rather than a nicety.
\end{itemize}
