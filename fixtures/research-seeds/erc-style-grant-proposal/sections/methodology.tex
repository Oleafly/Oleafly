runs a node that never exports records. The encoder is the mechanism from \ref{obj:encoding}, the

\subsection{Where the error enters}

Let $\alpha$ be the false match rate and $\beta$ the missed match rate of a linkage, and let $Z$
denote the demographic variables that drive both the linkage and the outcome model. Writing the
target estimand as $\theta$ and the naive linked-data estimator as $\hat{\theta}_{\text{naive}}$,
the first-order bias takes the form
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:bias}
  \mathbb{E}\bigl[\hat{\theta}_{\text{naive}}\bigr] - \theta
    = \alpha\,\bigl(\theta_{\text{false}} - \theta\bigr)
    + \operatorname{Cov}\!\left(\beta(Z),\, \frac{\partial \theta(Z)}{\partial Z}\right) + o(\alpha + \bar{\beta}).
\end{equation}
The first term is the one the applied literature expects and it is usually small. The second term
is the one that matters, because $\beta(Z)$ is large exactly where the population is unusual, and
it does not vanish as the sample grows. \ref{obj:theory} makes Equation~\eqref{eq:bias} sharp and
computable from certificate quantities.

\subsection{Encoding}

We construct a randomised sketch of the $q$-gram set of each identifier field, calibrated so that
the Hamming distance between two sketches is an unbiased estimator of the Jaccard distance between
the underlying sets, with noise chosen to satisfy $\varepsilon$-differential privacy at the record
level. The construction differs from a Bloom filter in that the noise is added to the counts rather
than to the membership bits, which is what lets the privacy loss be accounted rather than
estimated. The evaluation runs the published graph alignment attack against our encodings and
reports recovery rate as a function of $\varepsilon$, side information, and population size.

\subsection{Validation strategy}

Every claim is validated at three levels: on synthetic populations where the truth is known, on a
national registry pair for which a clerical review gold standard exists, and in the live
three-custodian deployment where only the certificate is available. The third level cannot validate
the bounds, so its role is to show that the certificate quantities are stable and that the protocol
completes within its time budget.
