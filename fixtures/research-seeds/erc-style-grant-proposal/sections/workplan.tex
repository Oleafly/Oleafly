\section{Work plan}
\label{sec:workplan}

\subsection{Work packages}

\workpackage{wp:theory}{Theory of linkage error propagation}{M1 to M30, lead: PI}
Sharp bounds for Equation~\eqref{eq:bias} under regression, Cox, and inverse-probability-weighting
estimands. Identification of the certificate quantities that a protocol must report for the bounds
to be computable. Deliverables: D1.1 bound theory report (M14), D1.2 diagnostic software (M26),
D1.3 validation against three gold-standard studies (M30). Serves \ref{obj:theory}.

\workpackage{wp:encoding}{Attack-resistant encodings}{M1 to M36, lead: postdoc 1}
Sketch construction, privacy proof, parameter accounting under composition across fields and across
custodians. Adversarial evaluation against frequency, pattern-mining, and graph alignment attacks.
Deliverables: D2.1 construction and proof (M18), D2.2 adversarial evaluation report (M30),
D2.3 reference implementation (M36). Serves \ref{obj:encoding}.

\workpackage{wp:protocol}{Federated protocol and implementation}{M7 to M48, lead: software engineer}
Secure comparison protocol, scheduling and blocking under encryption, certificate generation, and
the engineering needed to run 8 million records per custodian. Deliverables: D3.1 protocol
specification (M20), D3.2 single-custodian prototype (M30), D3.3 three-custodian deployment (M46).
Serves \ref{obj:protocol}, and depends on D2.1 from \ref{wp:encoding}.

\workpackage{wp:inference}{Inference under uncertain linkage}{M13 to M54, lead: postdoc 2}
Estimators that treat links as latent, with correct coverage under the certificate. Extension to
survival outcomes and to the federated case where the analyst never sees a link. Deliverables:
D4.1 estimator family and theory (M28), D4.2 software release (M40), D4.3 three published
reanalyses (M54). Serves \ref{obj:inference}, and depends on D1.1 from \ref{wp:theory}.

\workpackage{wp:governance}{Governance, ethics, and dissemination}{M1 to M60, lead: PI and legal adviser}
Data access agreements with three custodians, the ethics framework, the public engagement
programme, and all dissemination. Deliverables: D5.1 tripartite access agreement (M12),
D5.2 governance model paper (M24), D5.3 open release of all software and the final synthesis (M60).

\noindent
Figure~\ref{fig:gantt} shows two deliberate design choices. \ref{wp:theory} and \ref{wp:encoding}
both start in month 1, because neither can be specified without the other's vocabulary. And
\ref{wp:governance} runs the full 60 months, because the access agreement is the single item whose
delay would stall everything downstream of month 30.
