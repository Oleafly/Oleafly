\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sysname{} shows that per-prompt block selection is affordable. A rank-4 probe
costing 1.5 percent of the dense kernel recovers enough structure to cut
attention traffic by more than half at a 64k context, and a residual guard
keeps the failure mode observable rather than silent. The clearest direction
for future work is composing the fetch plan with key-value eviction, so that a
block skipped at prefill is also a block never retained.
