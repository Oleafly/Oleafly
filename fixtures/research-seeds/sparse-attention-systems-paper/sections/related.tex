\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Fixed sparsity.}
Sparse Transformers~\cite{child2019sparse}, Longformer~\cite{beltagy2020longformer},
and BigBird~\cite{zaheer2020bigbird} fix the pattern at model-design time and
retrain around it. They are strictly cheaper to schedule than \sysname{} and
strictly less adaptive; our measurements in Section~\ref{sec:background}
quantify the gap.

\paragraph{Kernel-level optimisation.}
FlashAttention~\cite{dao2022flashattention} and its successors reduce the
constant factor of dense attention by restructuring memory access. That work is
complementary: \sysname{} emits a fetch plan that such a kernel consumes.

\paragraph{Cache compression.}
Key-value eviction policies discard entries after the fact, whereas \sysname{}
avoids fetching them. The two compose, and the combination is the natural next
experiment.
