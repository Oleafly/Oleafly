\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Phase noise in a hybrid millimeter-wave receiver is common to the whole array
and therefore immune to beamforming gain, and at wideband symbol durations its
intercarrier leakage is structured rather than white. We derived that structure
exactly, without a small-phase approximation, and showed that the leakage
covariance is effectively rank $2\lceil \beta T_s N \rceil + 1$. \est{} turns
that fact into a variational estimator whose channel update is a fixed linear
solve and whose phase update is a scalar Kalman smoother, at a cost of 3.4
times the common-phase baseline and within 1.1~dB of a genie receiver across a
two-decade linewidth sweep.

The remaining work is the prior. A free-running Wiener model is the honest
choice for the oscillators these receivers actually ship, but locked
synthesizers are common in infrastructure equipment and they violate it in a
way that costs a third of the gain. The same smoother accepts a second-order
autoregressive prior with no change to the channel update, and fitting that
prior online from the residuals is the direction we are pursuing.
