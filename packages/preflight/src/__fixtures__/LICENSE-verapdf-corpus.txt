What they test
  Both files are tagged and declare pdfuaid:part 1 in their XMP metadata.
  The pass file sets DisplayDocTitle to true in ViewerPreferences. The fail
  file omits that entry, which is the veraPDF PDF/UA-1 rule 7.1-10 failure
  case. Preflight uses them to prove that its own DisplayDocTitle and
  PDF/UA claim checks read real files correctly, not just mocked pdf.js
  objects.
