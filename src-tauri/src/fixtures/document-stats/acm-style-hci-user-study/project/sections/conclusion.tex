\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Compile notifications are a solved problem in every editor and, on this
evidence, solved wrongly. Delivering a build result as an immediate dismissable
toast doubles resumption lag against an ambient in-place marker, adds 18
percent to task completion time, and raises self-reported mental demand by 21
points, while carrying exactly the same information. It is also worse than
saying nothing at all, which is the result that convinced us the mechanism is
attention rather than information.

The fix is cheap and the rule is simple: notify ambiently above roughly ten
seconds of build time, immediately below it. We are releasing the
instrumentation harness so that the measurement can be repeated in production
editors, where the interesting open question lives: whether the effect survives
a working day full of competing interruptions, or whether it is swamped by
them.
