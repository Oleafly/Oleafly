\section{Monthly measurement record}
\label{sec:measurement-table}

Table~\ref{tab:monthly-record} gives the monthly aggregate record for every
tower in the network. Air temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, net
radiation, sensible heat flux, and latent heat flux are monthly means over the
level 0 and level 1 records. Carbon flux is the monthly mean over the gap filled
record and therefore covers the full month in every case. Precipitation is the
monthly total. Data return is the percentage of half hours reaching level 1 or
better before gap filling.

The table is the primary machine readable product of this report and is
reproduced here so that the aggregates quoted elsewhere can be checked without
recourse to the archive. The rows in which data return falls below 70 percent
are the February storm outage described in Section~\ref{sec:network-return}, the
September datalogger fault at MC02 and MC04, and the recurring analyser
contamination at MC06 and MC07.

Three features of Table~\ref{tab:monthly-record} are worth drawing out. The
saltmarsh sites carry a latent heat flux roughly twice that of the dune sites in
every month of the growing season, which is the Bowen ratio difference reported
in Table~\ref{tab:annual-budget} resolved in time. The carbon flux at the dune
sites remains positive, that is a net source, in every month except June and
July, whereas the saltmarsh sites are a sink from April through September. And
the data return at MC06 and MC07 is below the network mean in eleven months of
twelve, which is the pattern that motivated
Recommendation~\ref{rec:enclosure} rather than any single outage.
