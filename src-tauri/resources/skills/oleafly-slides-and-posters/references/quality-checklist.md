# Quality checklist

Run this against the rendered PDF, not the source. Mark anything you could not check.

## Structure

- [ ] The title states the finding, not the topic.
- [ ] Every slide has one message, and that message is the frame title.
- [ ] The contribution is stated in the first two minutes.
- [ ] The result the talk is about appears before the halfway point.
- [ ] There is a conclusion slide with a takeaway, not just "Thank you".
- [ ] Backup slides are after the end, and marked.

## Timing

- [ ] Slide count matches the time available (see `assets/timing.md`).
- [ ] Data-heavy slides are counted as two.
- [ ] Section dividers and the title slide are not counted as content.
- [ ] The results section gets the largest share of the time.

## Legibility

- [ ] No slide has more than six lines of text.
- [ ] Bullets are phrases, not sentences.
- [ ] No font smaller than the theme default, except in a citation line.
- [ ] Text inside every figure is readable at the size it appears on the slide.
- [ ] No content runs past the bottom of a frame.
- [ ] Tables have at most four columns.

## Figures

- [ ] Every figure is referred to by the speaker's line, not left to speak for itself.
- [ ] Axes are labelled with units.
- [ ] Colour is not the only encoding.
- [ ] The palette is colour-blind safe.
- [ ] Figures from the paper have been checked at slide size, not assumed to work.
- [ ] Plots are vector, not upscaled raster.

## Poster only

- [ ] Board size confirmed with the venue.
- [ ] Body text at 24 pt or larger at A0 scale.
- [ ] Total body text under 800 words.
- [ ] Reading order is unambiguous.
- [ ] The main result figure is the largest element.
- [ ] White space is around half the board.
- [ ] QR code present, at least 5 cm, with a caption.
- [ ] Contact details on the poster.

## Project state

- [ ] The deck or poster compiled without errors.
- [ ] The rendered pages were looked at.
- [ ] The project's main document is back to the paper.
